About DownCity Band!!

DownCity Band brings the energy, talent, and fun to make your wedding unforgettable—one packed dance floor at a time.

DownCity Band is a premier live music entertainment service based in Providence, Rhode Island, specializing in unforgettable wedding celebrations. For more than 10 years, our eight-member ensemble—featuring some of the area’s top vocalists and musicians—has been filling dance floors, inspiring sing-alongs, and turning special moments into lifelong memories.

Our Music

From soulful blues and timeless classics to country, hip-hop, pop, and rock anthems, our repertoire spans a wide range of genres to make sure there’s something for everyone. Whether you want elegant instrumentals for your ceremony, a heartfelt first dance, or high-energy hits to keep the party going all night, we’ll deliver the perfect soundtrack for your big day. And if you have a special request, we’re happy to learn new songs to make your celebration uniquely yours.

More Than Just a Band

DownCity Band doesn’t just perform—we create an experience. Along with live music, we can provide music during breaks, act as your MC, and ensure that every transition of your celebration flows seamlessly. Our goal is to keep the energy high and your planning stress-free.

Why Couples Love Us

Couples consistently rave about our enthusiasm, professionalism, and easygoing style. Planning is simple, communication is clear, and when the big day comes, we bring the energy that gets everyone on the dance floor. It’s no surprise we were named 2019 Best of Newport County—an honor that reflects both our talent and our commitment to customer service.

Experience the joy and energy we bring to every performance, then imagine that same magic creating unforgettable memories at your own event.
