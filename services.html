Our Services

Unforgettable live music and seamless event production tailored to your celebration.

Wedding Celebrations

Your wedding deserves a soundtrack as unique as your love story. DownCity Band delivers full-day musical coverage—from intimate ceremony instrumentals to an electric, packed-dance-floor reception. We work closely with couples to tailor setlists, learn special first-dance requests, and ensure your big day flows effortlessly.

Ceremony & Cocktail Music

Set the mood before the party even starts. We offer solo and small-ensemble configurations (piano, acoustic guitar, or saxophone) to provide elegant classical, jazz, or pop arrangements for your guest arrival, ceremony processional, and cocktail hour.

Corporate Events & Galas

Elevate your company’s next celebration, fundraiser, or annual gala. DownCity Band brings high-energy versatility and polished professionalism to corporate audiences, delivering everything from background dinner jazz to top-40 and classic dance hits that bring team members together.

Private Parties & Venues

From milestone birthday bashes and anniversary parties to summer festivals and public tavern shows, our 8-piece ensemble brings non-stop energy, full brass sound, and infectious vocal performances that turn any gathering into a party.

MC & Sound Production

We do more than play music—we keep your event running on time and stress-free. All packages include professional sound gear, stage lighting, continuous background music during band breaks, and clear, engaging MC services for grand entrances, toasts, and announcements.
